Design system

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

The visual system of a small physiotherapy clinic's booking app: kinesiology tape on plaster, for patients choosing a time and staff reading the day. See README.md.

Foundations 6

Colour Warm plaster surfaces, ink edges, and one coral strip per screen. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Focus One ink ring on every control, drawn outside it. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Motion Quick and plain, with one press-on for the strip. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Shape Flat surfaces with edges, because plaster and tape have edges, not glows. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Spacing Large and plain on patient screens, dense on the staff list. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Type The time is the largest text on each patient screen, set in the device font. Draft updated 2026-11-01

Components 11

App frame The product root, the app bar, the page, and its headings. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Buttons Ink for actions, quiet for supporting ones, wine for the one irreversible step. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Week and days One week, always visible, with its month and a way to the next. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Day list The staff list in time order, where the next session is the only coloured row. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Fields A label above, a hint or a message below, and what was typed always kept. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Icons A stroke in the text colour, always beside a word. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Text links A small underlined action inside a line, 44 px tall. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Loading A spinner with words for a wait, and bars where rows will land. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Notices A block that says what happened, and a word that says it went well. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Time slot A free time is one row on the rail, read time first, and only the chosen slot wears the coral strip. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Appointment card The summary of a choice and the card of a confirmed booking. Draft updated 2026-11-01

Patterns 2

Empty, loading, and error states Every failure says what failed, what to do next, and what the person keeps. Draft updated 2026-11-01 Time picker A visible week, a rail of times, and a summary that names the choice. Draft updated 2026-11-01
