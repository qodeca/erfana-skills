Time picker

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

A patient picks a week, a day, and a time, in that order, and the time comes straight after the session because patients decide by when they can come. The same parts appear at every size and only the composition changes: the columns fold one at a time, four on a desktop, then three, two, and one.

When to use it

The situationUse
A new bookingThe time picker, after Choose a session
Moving a bookingThe same picker, under Move your booking
A typed dateNever: patients pick from a visible week

Every state

At each size

Width of .appSizeComposition
Under 37.5remPhoneOne pane. The week is seven cells, and .book__aside--dock is a bar across the bottom
37.5rem to 52.49remTabletTwo panes: the week as a list, with the times and summary as its detail
52.5rem to 74.99remTablet landscapeThree panes: the week, one column of times, and the summary
75rem and upDesktopFour columns: the week, morning, afternoon, and the summary
