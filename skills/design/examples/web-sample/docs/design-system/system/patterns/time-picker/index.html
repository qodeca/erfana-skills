Time picker

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

A patient picks a week, a day, and a time, in that order, and the time comes straight after the session because patients decide by when they can come. On a wide screen the times fold into two columns and the summary moves beside them.

When to use it

The situationUse
A new bookingThe time picker, after Choose a session
Moving a bookingThe same picker, under Move your booking
A typed dateNever: patients pick from a visible week
