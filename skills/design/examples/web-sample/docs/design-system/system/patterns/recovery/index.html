Empty, loading, and error states

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Every failure keeps the booking as it was. Each state says what failed, what to do next, and what the person keeps, and the clinic phone is always the fallback for a patient.

When to use it

The situationUse
Nothing to show on this dayA neutral notice and the way out
Times or rows on their wayWords with a spinner, and bars in place of the rows
A refresh in progressThe old rows stay, with a line above them
A step failedAn error notice, then Try again, then what is kept
Not allowedA neutral notice with the reason and the way in
