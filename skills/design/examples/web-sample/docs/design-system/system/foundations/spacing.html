Spacing

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Patient screens stay large and plain, because many patients book one-handed or in pain (inferred). Controls are 52 px tall, or 44 px for compact buttons and links, against the 24 px web minimum. Day cells measure 45 px wide at a 375 px width and 37 px at 320 px, both above the minimum.

Breakpoints

Breakpoints sit inside @container rules as rem values, because CSS cannot read a variable there: 56rem opens the wide layout, and 39.99rem folds the day list.
