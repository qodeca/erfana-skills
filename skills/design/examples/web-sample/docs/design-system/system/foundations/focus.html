Focus

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Every control shows the same ring: an outline in the focus colour, one edge wide and one edge clear of the control. It measures 14.53:1 on the light surface and 16.50:1 on the dark one, against the 3:1 it needs.
