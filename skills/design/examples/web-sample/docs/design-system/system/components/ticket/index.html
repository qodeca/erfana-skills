Appointment card

Draft updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

The time is the largest thing on the card. Coral marks what is picked, next, or confirmed, so here the strip marks a confirmed booking: it stays on that booking's card in later states until the session passes, and a card for an unsaved time never has it. A cancelled card stays as a record, struck through and with its strip peeled off.
