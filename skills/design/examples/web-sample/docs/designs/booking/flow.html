Choose a time, confirm, manage

A patient books a session, then moves or cancels it from the same booked screen. Judge whether the time is always the first thing read, and whether the one coral strip marks the right thing.

Open questions
  • Assumed: no account. A patient gives a name and a mobile number, and a text message carries the link back. To confirm with the clinic.
  • Assumed: the patient picks a first visit or a follow-up before choosing a time, with a first visit at 60 minutes and a follow-up at 45. To confirm with the clinic.
  • Assumed: any free therapist is fine, so the name sits beside the time and there is no therapist filter. To confirm.
  • Assumed: online booking opens 6 weeks ahead, and online changes close 24 hours before a session. Both numbers are guesses. The clinic decides.
  • Assumed: a text link stops working a set time after the session, and shows no details once expired. The period is open.
  • Assumed: a cancelled session costs nothing. If a late fee exists, the cancel state must say so.
  • Assumed: clinic name, phone number, and address are placeholders. Real ones change line lengths on the card.
  • Assumed: English only, 24-hour times. Other languages are not drawn; labels wrap rather than clip.
