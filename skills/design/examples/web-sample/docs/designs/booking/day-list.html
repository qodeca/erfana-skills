Staff day list

Reception and therapists read today's sessions at a glance between patients. Desktop at the desk is the main view, and the next session is its own pane at every size. Judge whether "who is next" reads from across the desk, and whether free gaps stand out from booked rows.

Open questions
  • Assumed: the list is read-only, because the brief says staff "see" it. Marking a patient as arrived would add a control to every row.
  • Assumed: one list for the whole clinic, with a therapist column. A per-therapist view is not drawn.
  • Assumed: the mobile column folds away on an upright tablet and a phone, and stays on a landscape tablet, and the next patient's number stays on their card at every size, so reception can call a late patient. To confirm against the clinic's privacy rules.
  • Assumed: a therapist may read the list on a tablet between sessions. Whether the clinic owns tablets is not known.
  • Assumed: a tablet is touch only. A tablet with a keyboard or mouse would need the desktop hover and focus states.
  • Assumed: "Next" is the first booked session after the current time, and the current time here is 10:40.
Sizes

How this adapts: Each state is drawn desktop, tablet, tablet landscape, then phone. The list reads the width of its own pane: six columns on a desktop, five on a landscape tablet, and stacked rows on an upright tablet and a phone. The next session keeps its own pane, and on a phone it comes first and the day buttons drop to the bottom.

  • A tablet held upright is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state default.
  • A tablet on its side is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state default.
  • A phone is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state default.
  • A tablet held upright shows the layout of the state default, with one line added above the rows, as the desktop and phone frames of this state show.
  • A tablet on its side shows the layout of the state default, with one line added above the rows, as the desktop and phone frames of this state show.
