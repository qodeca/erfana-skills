Staff day list

Reception and therapists read today's sessions at a glance between patients. Judge whether "who is next" reads from across the desk, and whether free gaps stand out from booked rows.

Open questions
  • Assumed: the list is read-only, because the brief says staff "see" it. Marking a patient as arrived would add a control to every row.
  • Assumed: one list for the whole clinic, with a therapist column. A per-therapist view is not drawn.
  • Assumed: the mobile number is shown so reception can call a late patient. It folds away on a phone. To confirm against the clinic's privacy rules.
  • Assumed: staff sign in, and a signed-out person sees the refused state. How they sign in is not designed here.
  • Assumed: "Next" is the first booked session after the current time, and the current time here is 10:40.
