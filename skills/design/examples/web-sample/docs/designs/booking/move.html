Move a booking

A move runs the same week and rail as a new booking. Judge whether the old booking stays clear until the new one is saved.

Open questions
  • Assumed: a tablet is touch only. A tablet with a keyboard or mouse would need the desktop hover and focus states.
  • Assumed: the two tablet sizes are one 768 by 1024 screen, upright and on its side. Widths between the drawn sizes take the layout below them, and a phone on its side, which is short rather than narrow, is not drawn.
  • Assumed: online changes close 24 hours before a session. The number is a guess. The clinic decides.
  • Assumed: a moved booking keeps its session type.
Sizes

How this adapts: Each state is drawn desktop, tablet, tablet landscape, then phone. By width, the picker has four columns on a desktop, three panes on a landscape tablet, two on an upright tablet, and one on a phone, where the week becomes seven cells and the actions move to the bottom.

  • A tablet held upright is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state move-choose.
  • A tablet on its side is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state move-choose.
  • A phone is drawn as touch only, so it has no hover or keyboard focus state. Its layout is in the state move-choose.
