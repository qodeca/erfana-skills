Confirm: sending and failures

Each failure keeps what was typed. Judge whether the patient can always tell that nothing has been booked.

Open questions
  • Assumed: a tablet is touch only. A tablet with a keyboard or mouse would need the desktop hover and focus states.
  • Assumed: the two tablet sizes are one 768 by 1024 screen, upright and on its side. Widths between the drawn sizes take the layout below them, and a phone on its side, which is short rather than narrow, is not drawn.
  • Assumed: a taken time is known only after pressing "Book this time". To confirm with the booking system.
Sizes

How this adapts: Each state is drawn desktop, tablet, tablet landscape, then phone. By width, a form has its fields in a row beside the card on a desktop, three panes on a landscape tablet, two on an upright tablet, and one on a phone, where the card folds and sits with the actions at the bottom.

Every state on this page is drawn at every size, so no exception is recorded.
