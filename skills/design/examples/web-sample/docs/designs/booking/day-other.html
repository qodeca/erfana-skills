Staff day list: other days

Staff look ahead and back between patients. Judge whether another day can ever be mistaken for today.

Open questions
  • Assumed: a tablet is touch only. A tablet with a keyboard or mouse would need the desktop hover and focus states.
  • Assumed: a past day keeps its list, with no record of who attended, because the list is read-only.
Sizes

How this adapts: Each state is drawn desktop, tablet, tablet landscape, then phone. The list reads the width of its own pane: six columns on a desktop, five on a landscape tablet, and stacked rows on an upright tablet and a phone. The next session keeps its own pane, and on a phone it comes first and the day buttons drop to the bottom.

Every state on this page is drawn at every size, so no exception is recorded.
