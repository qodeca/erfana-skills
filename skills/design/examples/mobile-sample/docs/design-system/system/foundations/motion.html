Design system / Foundations / Motion

Motion

In review updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Motion only confirms that a tap landed. Press feedback takes 100 ms, sheets 300 ms in and 200 ms out, and nothing plays for show. With reduced motion on, movement goes and every state change stays.
