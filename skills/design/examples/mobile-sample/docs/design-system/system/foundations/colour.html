Colour

In review updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Warm sand neutrals and one marigold action colour, the same in both themes. If something on a normal screen is filled marigold, it is the next thing to tap. Red and green only ever repeat a word or an icon.

Surfaces in both themes

Measured contrast

Measured from tokens.json with the WCAG 2.2 formula when this page was built. Every text and control pair below passes in both themes. Two colour pairs stay under 3:1 in the light theme: the day band on a raised surface, at 1.78:1, and a skeleton on a raised surface, at 1.34:1. They are listed rather than left out, and they are allowed because neither carries meaning: the word Day and the sun icon on every ticket say the same as the band, and the words beside a skeleton say what is loading.

Exceptions

Two pairs stay under 3:1 in the light theme: the day band on a raised surface, at 1.78:1, and a skeleton on a raised surface, at 1.34:1. Neither carries meaning, so both are allowed. A skeleton on a side pane, at 1.51:1, and the hairline between panes, at 1.25:1, are the same kind of decoration: the panes also differ by position, and a list or side pane by its background. Details are under Measured pairs.
