Screen, app bar, action bar, and sheet

In review updated 2026-11-02 · Design, not a working product

The frame of every screen. The main action lives in a pinned bar at the bottom, in thumb reach. Sheets rise from the bottom and are drawn open on review pages. On every sheet the safe action sits at the bottom, in thumb reach. From 600 the action bar becomes a row and a sheet stops growing: it is centred at 480, and on Android stays at the bottom, up to 640. Panes draws both in place, and the sizes are drawn in full on the screen pages.
