Notice

In review updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

Says what happened, what to do, and what is kept. A notice never disappears on its own, because a tired person may look away.

Every state

Keyboard and screen reader

A phone has no keyboard of its own, so this covers a paired keyboard and the double-tap of VoiceOver and TalkBack.
