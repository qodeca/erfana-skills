Loading and keeping data

In review updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

What is already known stays on screen, marked with its age if it may be old. Skeletons match the shape that is coming, and a word sits beside every spinner.

When to use it

The situationUse
Nothing is known yetFirst load: skeletons in the shape that is coming
Something is already on screenReload: keep it, with a thin line of status above
