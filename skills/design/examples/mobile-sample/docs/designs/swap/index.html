Swap

In review updated 2026-11-01 · Design, not a working product

A nurse offers a shift, a colleague takes it, and a manager approves. Design system 3.1.0.

  • Offer a shift – judge whether a tired nurse can finish this in under a minute
  • Your offer – judge whether a glance says who holds the shift
  • Accept a shift – judge whether a colleague can say yes or no safely, from a notification
  • Swap requests – judge whether a manager can decide safely in under a minute
  • Large text – judge the three busiest screens and both sheets at 200% text
