Large text

The three busiest screens and both sheets with the text size at 200%, drawn with the large-text mode, on a phone and on a tablet both ways round. On a tablet 200% text takes panes away, so reading comes first. Tired eyes and bigger system text are normal here, and nothing may scroll sideways.

Open questions
  • Assumed: 200% is the largest text size to support. iOS and Android accessibility sizes can go further, so the real limit needs testing on a device.
  • Assumed: at 200% a tablet drops to two equal columns, and Pick a shift to one pane with tickets across. The real switch point needs testing on a device.
  • Decided here, reversible: holds last half a second at least. They are Approve swap after a sheet closes or a request opens, Send offer on a reopened draft, a decline sheet’s buttons as the keyboard moves it, and a commit at 200% text. The length is a stand-in. To confirm on a device.
  • Not confirmed: Android’s sheet limit of 640. No page read gave the number. To confirm with engineering.
Sizes
  • Every state is drawn at every size, because large text changes the layout at each one.
