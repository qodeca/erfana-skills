Large text

The three busiest screens and both sheets with the phone’s text size at 200%, drawn with the large-text mode. Tired eyes and bigger system text are normal here, and nothing may scroll sideways.

Open questions
  • Assumed: 200% is the largest text size to support. iOS and Android accessibility sizes can go further, so the real limit needs testing on a device.
