Your offer

After sending, the nurse checks one thing, often from a notification: who holds this shift now. In each pair the light theme is on the left and the dark theme on the right. Frames show the layout iOS and Android share. Where the two differ, each is drawn as its own labelled state under iOS and Android differences.

Open questions
  • Assumed: the nurse stays on the shift until the manager approves. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Assumed: an open offer ends when the cut-off arrives, and nobody has to do anything. To confirm.
  • Assumed: a manager decides by the cut-off, 24 hours before the shift, and missing it ends the offer. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Assumed: a manager must give a reason to decline, and the nurse sees it word for word. To confirm.
  • Names, wards, and hours are invented. The longest real name on staff should replace them.
