Your offer

After sending, the nurse checks one thing, often from a notification: who holds this shift now. Each state is drawn for phone, then tablet, then tablet landscape, and dark frames follow in the same group. Sizes with nothing new are listed under Sizes, with the reason. Frames show the layout iOS and Android share. Where the two differ, each is drawn as its own labelled state under iOS and Android differences.

Open questions
  • Assumed: the nurse stays on the shift until the manager approves. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Assumed: an open offer ends when the cut-off arrives, and nobody has to do anything. To confirm.
  • Assumed: a manager decides by the cut-off, 24 hours before the shift, and missing it ends the offer. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Assumed: a manager must give a reason to decline, and the nurse sees it word for word. To confirm.
  • Names, wards, and hours are invented. The longest real name on staff should replace them.
  • Assumed: a nurse reads this on their own tablet as well as their phone, and the shift list stays beside the offer. On a shared ward tablet, this page shows a named colleague and a manager’s words to whoever is nearby. To confirm with HR.
  • Assumed: My shifts here is the same list as on Offer a shift, and an approved shift leaves it at once, while its detail stays open. To confirm with a nurse.
  • Assumed: a tablet offers Message and shows the number, because many tablets cannot call. Which messaging app it opens, and whether a tablet that can call should still call, are open. To confirm with IT.
  • Assumed: tapping a shift with no offer from Your offer opens step 2 of Offer a shift, and this offer is unchanged. To confirm with a nurse.
  • Assumed: rotating a tablet mid-task keeps the open item, any typed text, and any open sheet. To confirm with engineering.
  • Assumed: two panes at 768, though Android’s guide shows one pane at this width. To confirm on a real tablet.
  • Assumed: touch only on tablets, with no pointer, so no hover is drawn. A paired keyboard or trackpad on an iPad may need one. To confirm with the ward team.
  • Assumed: the action bar under the main pane is reachable when a tablet is propped in landscape or held in two hands. Not tested. To confirm on a device.
  • Not confirmed: Android’s sheet limit of 640. No page read gave the number. To confirm with engineering.

How this screen adapts

SizeLayoutMain action
PhoneEverything in one column, opened from the listAt the bottom, in reach of one thumb
TabletMy shifts beside the offer, with the baton under itA bar under the main pane, with the main button at the inline end. A step that leads to a commit stays out of that commit’s slot
Tablet landscapeMy shifts, the offer, and the batonA bar under the main pane, with the main button at the inline end. A step that leads to a commit stays out of that commit’s slot

On a phone the action bar is drawn at the end of the content, because a review page hides nothing. In the product it is pinned to the bottom of the screen.

Tapping a shift with no offer opens step 2 of Offer a shift, and this offer does not change. Tapping another offered shift opens that offer here. Offer it again and Choose someone else both open step 2 for this shift. Done closes the open pane and leaves the list.
