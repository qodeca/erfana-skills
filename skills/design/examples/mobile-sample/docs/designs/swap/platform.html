iOS and Android differences

Where the two platforms really differ, each is drawn as its own labelled state, at phone, tablet, and tablet landscape: the font, the app bar, and the sheet. Everything else in this product is shared.

Open questions
  • Decided here, reversible: holds last half a second at least. They are Approve swap after a sheet closes or a request opens, Send offer on a reopened draft, a decline sheet’s buttons as the keyboard moves it, and a commit at 200% text. The length is a stand-in. To confirm on a device.
  • Not seen: real Android type. Review pages ship no font, so without Roboto installed a Mac shows Android frames in a fallback. Check Android type on a device.
  • Not confirmed: Android’s sheet limit of 640. No page read gave the number. To confirm with engineering.
  • Assumed: two panes at 768, though Android’s guide shows one pane at this width. To confirm on a real tablet.
  • Assumed: touch only on tablets, with no pointer, so no hover is drawn. A paired keyboard or trackpad on an iPad may need one. To confirm with the ward team.
  • Assumed: the action bar under the main pane is reachable when a tablet is propped in landscape or held in two hands. Not tested. To confirm on a device.
  • Assumed: iPadOS shows this sheet as a form sheet, centred, with no grabber, because it does not resize. To confirm on a device.
Sizes
  • Every state is drawn at every size, because the platforms differ at each one.
