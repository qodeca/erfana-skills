Offer a shift

A nurse, often tired and between tasks, picks a shift, chooses who gets the offer, and sends it. In each pair the light theme is on the left and the dark theme on the right. Frames show the layout iOS and Android share. Where the two differ, each is drawn as its own labelled state under iOS and Android differences.

Open questions
  • Assumed: offers close 24 hours before the shift starts. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Assumed: the rest rule is 11 hours between shifts. Local law and union agreements may differ.
  • Assumed: over weekly hours and too little rest both rule a colleague out, and no manager can override either. Whether a manager may override hours stays out until HR and the union decide.
  • Not seen: real Android type. Review pages ship no font, so without Roboto installed a Mac shows Android frames in a fallback. Check Android type on a device.
  • Assumed: colleagues see only a general reason such as "Needs more rest", never leave or health details. To confirm with HR.
  • Assumed: a nurse may offer to one person or to everyone who can take it, and the first to accept takes it. To confirm with the ward team.
  • Assumed: the nurse stays on the shift until the manager approves. To confirm with nursing management.
  • Names, wards, and hours are invented. The longest real name on staff should replace them.
