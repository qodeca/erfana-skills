# Interview – Anna Kowalska, CTO

Date: 2026-09-14

**Q: What is the budget for the platform migration?**
A: We approved 750,000 EUR for the whole migration, spread over two financial years.

**Q: How long will the migration take?**
A: The plan is six months from kickoff. Kickoff is planned for January.

**Q: When do you expect the new platform to go live?**
A: Go-live is planned for Q3 2027. Q2 was discussed but rejected because of the summer freeze.

**Q: Which countries are in scope?**
A: Poland and Czechia in the first wave. Slovakia comes later, not in this project.

**Q: Who owns the vendor relationship?**
A: I do. The CFO only signs the contract.

**Q: What worries you most?**
A: Honestly, data quality. Our product master data has a lot of duplicates. I said to the board: "If we migrate dirty data, we just move the mess to a more expensive place."
