# Sources

1. Northfield Workforce Survey 2026 – https://example.org/northfield-2026 – reports that 58% of mid-sized companies run a fixed hybrid schedule (two or three office days).
2. Carlow Institute, "Flexible work and retention" (2026) – https://example.org/carlow-retention – reports that 58% of mid-sized companies run a fixed hybrid schedule, and that voluntary turnover fell by 28% in companies that kept flexible work for two years.
3. Brenner & Ito, "Output metrics in distributed teams" (2025) – https://example.org/brenner-ito – describes outcome-based goals as more reliable than activity tracking.
