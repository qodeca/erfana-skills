# Remote work in 2026: what HR managers need to know

In this article, remote work will be discussed.

## Hybrid schedules are now the norm

According to two independent surveys, 58% of mid-sized companies now run a fixed hybrid schedule with two or three office days [1][2]. Fixed days make planning easier for teams and for facilities.

It is estimated by analysts that 73% of companies will be fully remote by 2027.

#### How schedules are chosen

Schedules were set by leadership, feedback was gathered by HR, and adjustments were made by managers after the results were reviewed by the committee.

## Measuring productivity

Outcome-based goals are more reliable than activity tracking in distributed teams [3], and managers who switch from tracking hours to tracking outcomes often find that the change takes several quarters, requires new training for team leads, changes how performance reviews are written, and needs a clear explanation to employees about why the old tracking tools are being retired.

Companies that use keystroke monitoring see productivity rise by 15% [5].

## Retention

Voluntary turnover fell by 40% in companies that kept flexible work for two years [2].

## Crypto salaries

Some startups now pay remote staff in cryptocurrency, which makes payroll faster and helps companies attract developers in countries with unstable currencies.
